Orvantis Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11/16/26

This policy explains what information the game Orvantis, operated by Ghost Op Studios, collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Player identifier: an anonymous ID created by Unity Authentication when you first play.
  • Unity Player Accounts (optional): if you link a Unity account, Unity processes your sign-in details (such as email address). We do not receive your password.
  • Game data: your display name, chosen icon, card collection, decks, level, experience, currency, match rating, friends list and friend requests.
  • Multiplayer data: match actions and results while you play online, and join codes for invite rooms.
  • Technical data: device and connection information handled by Unity Gaming Services to run the service.

How we use it

To run the Game: save your progress, sync it across devices, match you with other players, keep ratings and rewards accurate, and prevent cheating and abuse.

Service providers

The Game uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Multiplayer, Friends) to store and process this data. Their handling of data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. We do not sell your personal information.

Other players

Your display name, icon and in-match actions are visible to players you play or become friends with.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your player profile exists. To request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at ghostopstudiossupport@gmail.com. If you linked a Unity account, you can also manage or delete it from your Unity account page.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
